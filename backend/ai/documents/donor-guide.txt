================================================================================
FOODRESCUE INDIA - DONOR GUIDE
================================================================================

1. WHO CAN BECOME A FOOD DONOR?
Any organization or individual with unserved, wholesome, edible food can register as a Food Donor on FoodRescue India. Common donor categories include:
- Restaurants, cafes, and diners
- Banquet halls, wedding planners, and caterers
- Corporate cafeterias and tech park dining centers
- Bakeries, patisseries, and dessert kitchens
- Hotels, resorts, and convention venues
- Supermarkets, grocery stores, and food distributors
- Event organizers and individual citizens

2. HOW TO REGISTER AS A DONOR:
- Click on "Register" or "Join as Donor" on the platform.
- Enter your full name, email address, password, phone number (+91 format), and organization name (e.g., "The Oberoi Grand Kitchen", "Royal Feast Caterers").
- Select your primary operating city (e.g., Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad).
- Provide your FSSAI registration/license number (optional, but highly recommended for commercial food businesses to enhance trust and verification).
- Submit the form to instantly access the Donor Portal.

3. HOW TO LIST A FOOD DONATION:
Step 1: Open the Donor Portal from the top navigation bar.
Step 2: Click the primary "+ List Food Surplus" button.
Step 3: Fill in the donation details:
  - Title: Clear, descriptive name (e.g., "40 Portions Paneer Butter Masala & Steamed Rice", "Fresh Assorted Breads & Rolls").
  - Food Category: Select from Cooked Meals, Bakery & Confectionery, Fresh Produce, Dairy Products, or Packaged Groceries.
  - Servings Count: Estimated number of persons this food can nourish (e.g., 50 servings).
  - Estimated Weight: Approximate total weight in kilograms (kg) (the system auto-estimates based on servings if left blank).
  - Dietary Type: Pure Vegetarian (Green indicator), Non-Vegetarian (Red indicator), or Egg-containing (Yellow indicator).
  - Storage Requirements: Ambient (room temperature), Refrigerated (chill 0°C to 4°C), or Frozen (below -18°C).
  - Preparation & Expiry: When the food was prepared and the safe pickup/consumption window (e.g., "Must be consumed within 4 hours").
  - Pickup Location: Accurate street address, landmark locality, city, and active contact phone number for the on-site dispatch coordinator.
  - Notes: Any special packing or allergen details (e.g., "Packed in sealed aluminium trays; please bring clean transport crates").
Step 4: Click "List Donation". Your donation is immediately broadcasted across the local NGO and volunteer network.

4. DONOR RESPONSIBILITIES & BEST PRACTICES:
- List food as early as possible. Do not wait until the final hour before closing to post surplus.
- Maintain thermal hygiene: keep hot foods hot (>60°C) and cold foods refrigerated (<5°C) until the volunteer or NGO arrives.
- Pack food in clean, food-grade, leak-proof containers or trays.
- Keep a staff member designated as the pickup point of contact with phone active.
- Verify the credentials of the receiving volunteer or NGO coordinator upon arrival before handing over the food parcels.
