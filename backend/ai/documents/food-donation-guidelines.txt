================================================================================
FOODRESCUE INDIA - FOOD DONATION GUIDELINES & ACCEPTABLE CATEGORIES
================================================================================

1. WHAT FOOD CAN BE DONATED?
FoodRescue India accepts wholesome, fresh, unconsumed, and properly stored food across five main categories:

A. Cooked Meals & Entrees:
- Freshly prepared curries, lentils (dal), rice, roti, chapati, pulao, biryani, and vegetable preparations.
- Must be surplus from unserved batches (e.g., buffet reserve pans that remained in clean warmers, catering trays untouched by guests).
- Must have been prepared within the last 2 to 4 hours under sanitary conditions.

B. Bakery & Confectionery:
- Bread loaves, buns, dinner rolls, croissants, bagels, and dry pastries.
- Must be fresh, unexpired, and free from moisture damage or mold.
- Cream-filled pastries or custard items require refrigeration and strict 24-hour windows.

C. Fresh Produce (Fruits & Vegetables):
- Whole, uncut fruits (apples, bananas, oranges, mangoes, etc.).
- Fresh vegetables (potatoes, onions, tomatoes, carrots, gourds, greens).
- Must be firm, clean, and free from rot, severe bruising, or insect infestation.

D. Dairy Products:
- Pasteurized milk pouches, paneer, curd, yogurt, butter, and cheese.
- Must be within manufacturer expiry/use-by dates and maintained under continuous refrigeration (0°C to 4°C).
- Packaging must be intact, sealed, and unopened.

E. Packaged & Dry Groceries:
- Grains, lentils, pulses, flour (atta/maida), cooking oils, spices, tea, sugar, biscuits, noodles, and canned goods.
- Must be in original, uncompromised factory packaging with legible expiry/best-before dates.

2. WHAT CANNOT BE DONATED (STRICTLY PROHIBITED):
- Plate scrapings, table leftovers, or food that has been partially consumed by customers or guests.
- Food displaying signs of spoilage, sour odors, mold, slime, or discoloration.
- Cooked food that has stayed in the danger temperature zone (5°C to 60°C) for over 4 hours.
- Opened or partially used commercial milk, yogurt, or canned goods.
- Alcohol or any products containing intoxicants or banned substances.
- Expired packaged products past their manufacturer-stated "Use By" dates.

3. QUANTITY THRESHOLDS:
- To make logistics viable for volunteer drivers and NGOs, minimum recommended donation size is:
  * Cooked Meals: At least 10 to 15 servings.
  * Packaged/Dry Goods: At least 5 kg total weight.
  * Bakery: At least 10 pieces or 2 loaves.
  * Produce: At least 5 kg.
There is no upper limit—bulk donations of hundreds of servings from banquets and corporate campuses are routinely redistributed.
