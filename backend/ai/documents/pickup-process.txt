================================================================================
FOODRESCUE INDIA - PICKUP PROCESS & LOGISTICS WORKFLOW
================================================================================

1. END-TO-END WORKFLOW SUMMARY:
The FoodRescue India pickup and delivery pipeline consists of 4 coordinated stages:

Stage 1: Listing & Immediate Broadcast
- The Donor creates a food listing via the Donor Portal.
- Status is set to "AVAILABLE".
- The listing immediately populates the NGO Portal and Impact Map.

Stage 2: Claiming by Verified NGO
- An eligible NGO identifies the surplus listing and clicks "Claim Donation".
- The NGO inputs destination shelter details and coordinator phone.
- Status shifts to "CLAIMED".
- The mission is published to the Volunteer Dispatch Board.

Stage 3: Volunteer Driver Assignment & Mission Acceptance
- An active Volunteer in the locality views the claimed donation and clicks "Accept Mission".
- Status shifts to "IN TRANSIT" / "ASSIGNED".
- Volunteer contact information and vehicle type are shared with both Donor and NGO.
- The volunteer proceeds to the donor kitchen.

Stage 4: Pickup, Transport, and Safe Handover
- Volunteer arrives at the donor facility, verifies packaging, and confirms pickup in the app ("Food Picked Up").
- Volunteer transports food directly to the shelter using insulated bags or boxes.
- Upon arrival at the shelter, the NGO verifies the items and accepts delivery.
- Volunteer clicks "Mark Completed / Delivered".
- Status shifts to "COMPLETED" and platform metrics (meals rescued, kg diverted) are credited to all parties.

2. ON-SITE PICKUP PROTOCOL FOR DRIVERS:
- Park safely in designated delivery/loading zones.
- Introduce yourself to the kitchen manager as a FoodRescue volunteer.
- Check that containers are sealed and steady before loading into the vehicle.
- Never open sealed food lids or taste food samples.
- Secure containers to prevent tipping during turns or braking.

3. HANDLING UNEXPECTED DELAYS OR EMERGENCIES:
- If running late due to traffic or vehicle breakdown:
  * Immediately call both the donor contact and NGO coordinator using the phone numbers provided in the mission view.
  * If the vehicle cannot proceed, notify platform support or re-broadcast the mission so a nearby volunteer driver can intercept and complete the delivery.
- If the donor is unreachable upon arrival:
  * Wait at the reception or loading dock for at least 15 minutes while attempting to call the registered coordinator.
- If the food is visibly spoiled or smells off upon pickup:
  * Politely inform the donor manager and do not accept the batch. Safety always takes precedence over volume.
